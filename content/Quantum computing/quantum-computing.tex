\begin{equation}
  \ket{+} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\ket{0}+\ket{1}\right).
  \label{eq:plus_state}
\end{equation}
I love the equation~\eqref{eq:plus_state} very much!

\begin{equation}
\ket{+} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{bmatrix}
    1 & 1 \\
    1 & -1
\end{bmatrix}\ket{0}.
\label{eq:plus_state_matrix}
\end{equation}

The equation~\eqref{eq:plus_state_matrix} shows how to create the plus state using a Hadamard gate. Both equations~\eqref{eq:plus_state} and~\eqref{eq:plus_state_matrix} describe the same quantum state.
