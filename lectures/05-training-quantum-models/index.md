## 🎯 Learning objectives

You should already be comfortable with:

- **Variational Algorithms (VQE & QAOA)**. The classical optimiser loop around VQE.

## 🗺️ What we cover

1. **Losses for quantum models**: MSE, cross-entropy and hinge on top of $\langle O\rangle$
2. **The parameter-shift rule**: exact gradients from two shifted circuits
3. **Simulator shortcuts**: backpropagation and adjoint differentiation
4. **Optimisers**: GD, Adam, SPSA and the quantum natural gradient
5. **The cost of a gradient**: $2P$ circuits × shots × data points

## ✏️ The one equation to remember

For a gate $e^{-i\theta P/2}$ generated by a Pauli operator $P$,

$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial \theta}
= \frac{f\!\left(\theta + \tfrac{\pi}{2}\right) - f\!\left(\theta - \tfrac{\pi}{2}\right)}{2}.
$$ (eq-parameter-shift)

Unlike a finite difference, the formula in [](#eq-parameter-shift) is **exact**, and it works on real hardware [@mitarai2018circuit; @schuld2019gradients].

## 🤔 Warm-up

A model has 50 parameters and you use 1000 shots per circuit. What does one parameter-shift gradient cost for a single data point?

:::{dropdown} Reveal the answer
$2 \times 50 \times 1000 = 100\,000$ shots. Multiply by the batch size and the number of epochs, and you can see why simulators, batching and smarter optimisers matter.
:::
