## 🎯 Learning objectives

- Pick a dataset, then design an encoding and ansatz for it
- Train and tune on a simulator within a fixed budget
- Compare against at least two classical baselines
- Report results with error bars across random seeds

## 🧩 Prerequisites

You should already be comfortable with:

- **Testing with pytest**. Test your data pipeline and model code.
- **GitHub**. Version the project and share it.

## 🧭 Milestones

1. **Proposal**: dataset, question and success metric, in half a page
2. **Baselines**: two tuned classical models with error bars
3. **Quantum model**: encoding, ansatz, readout and training loop
4. **Ablation**: change one design choice at a time and measure the effect
5. **Report**: a short MyST article with figures and a reproducible notebook
