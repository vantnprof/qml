## 🎯 Learning objectives

- Pose a precise, testable research question
- Reproduce a published QML result
- Extend it with at least one new experiment
- Publish the findings as an article in this book

## 🧩 Prerequisites

You should already be comfortable with:

- **GitHub Actions**. Rerun every experiment automatically.
- **Running on Real Quantum Hardware**. Optionally validate on real hardware.

## 🧭 Milestones

1. **Pick a paper**: suggestions: QRC for time series, QCNN phase classification, kernel concentration
2. **Reproduce**: match a key figure within error bars
3. **Extend**: a new dataset, noise model, ansatz or scale
4. **Stress-test**: seeds, baselines and a simulability check
5. **Publish**: an article and notebook merged into this book
